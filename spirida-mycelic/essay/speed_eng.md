# Conduction Velocity of Electrical Spikes in *Pleurotus djamor*

## References
* **FUNGAR Deliverable D4.1** – *Intrinsic spiking of electrical potential of mycelium*  
  20 mm electrode gap, response in 2–5 s → 4–10 mm s⁻¹
* **FUNGAR Deliverable D4.2** – *Catalogue of electrical activity patterns related to chemical and physical stimulation*  
  50 mN mechanical press, response in 6–12 s → 1.7–3.3 mm s⁻¹
* **Valverdu & Adamatzky (2022)** – *Propagation of electrical signals by fungi*  
  Slow metabolite waves 0.03 m h⁻¹ (0.008 mm s⁻¹); fast "gliders" 0.2–0.8 mm s⁻¹

These data points correspond to typical spike archetypes (S-α … S-δ) observed in **Pleurotus djamor**. A reasonable mean value and scaling per spike type are assumed in Table&nbsp;1.

---

## Table 1 – Instantaneous conduction velocity
| Spike type | Functional description        | Estimated speed (mm s⁻¹) |
|----------- |------------------------------|-------------------------|
| S-α        | Fast, narrow (information)   | **2.0** |
| S-β        | Medium, broad (flow)         | **0.8** |
| S-γ        | Paired/chaotic (storm)       | **3.0** |
| S-δ        | Burst / long-range (universal)| **0.2** |
