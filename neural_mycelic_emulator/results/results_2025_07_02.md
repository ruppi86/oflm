## Cohesive cross-species analysis (2025-07-03)

* **Best glyph distribution** per species: Cordyceps_large (0.266), Enoki_small (0.374), Ghost_medium (0.124), Schizo_large (0.212).
* **Temporal alignment** (highest KS p, lowest |d|): Ghost_medium & Schizo_large achieve p≥1.0 (perfect), Cordyceps_medium acceptable, Enoki all sizes show mismatch.
* **Silence ratio**: All large models now within ±0.01 of real data; Enoki remains elevated (0.11 vs 0.04–0.03 in others) indicating species-specific pattern.
* **Training efficiency**: Ghost datasets largest – even large model reached PPL ≈1.22 after 24 epochs; diminishing returns after ~15 epochs.
* **Model scaling effect**: Larger capacity helps Cordyceps & Schizo, mixed for Ghost (better rhythm, worse glyph) and hurts Enoki (overfits temporal pattern without fixing glyphs).

**Recommendation:**
1. Use *species-specific* best models above as baselines.
2. Investigate longer context windows (128) for Enoki & Ghost to see if glyph alignment improves without hurting rhythm.
3. Add ISI-matching auxiliary loss to further reduce KS stat where still significant.
4. Deploy deterministic seeding + patience-based early stopping to cut CPU time (~40 % speed-up observed in Ghost training).
